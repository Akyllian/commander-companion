# R2 Partita Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild the Partita screen to match the original mockup. Each player card shows identity, life ±1 and three entry points: Dettagli, Contatori, Danni Commander. Counters and Commander damage open inside the card. The central rhombus becomes a hub for Sezioni, Dadi, Mana and Moneta. Cards with no commander get a random Wizards illustration that stays fixed for the whole match.

**Architecture:**
- **Transient surface.** One pure, tested union in `src/match/surface.ts` (`closed | hub | tool | counters | commander`). It lives in `App` and replaces the R1 `revealed`-only model, so at most one surface is ever open.
- **Card.** `PlayerPanel` is rewritten around the new anatomy. The D-033 `QuickScoreDock` is deleted; its labels and source list move to a shared vocabulary module. The Contatori drawer and the Commander panel are siblings inside the card. They rotate with the seat because they belong to its transformed content. Compact mode comes from a size container query, not JS measurement.
- **Rhombus layer.** The hub is screen-oriented and non-modal. Its geometry (fit test, largest free quadrant, panel placement) is pure in `src/shell/hubGeometry.ts`.
- **Random art.** It lives in a small presentation envelope (`src/match/presentation.ts`, its own localStorage key, version 1), separate from the score history. Downloads go through the existing Scryfall adapter and IndexedDB art store.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, TypeScript 7, CSS (container queries, `@starting-style`, `transition-behavior: allow-discrete`, `inert`), Vitest, Testing Library, fast-check, localStorage, IndexedDB

**Spec:**
- Design (normative): `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-r2-match-design.md`, with the board `docs/art-direction/r2-match-board.svg`. Approved by the product owner on 2026-10-20; builder check passed.
- Product: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-app-restructure-design.md` (D-037). R1 shell: D-038.
- Kept unchanged: D-031 (contrast), D-034/D-035 (layouts), D-036 (cumulative delta and single announcement).
- Superseded: the D-033 dock and the R1 "rhombus = sections" behaviour (spec §14).

## Global Constraints

- All copy in Italian, using the spec's exact strings:
  - card: `Prendi Monarca`, `Rilascia Monarca`, `Apri dettagli di [nome]`, `Comandante non scelto`, `Contatori`, `Chiudi contatori`, `Danni Commander`, `Fuori dalla partita`;
  - Details: `Arte casuale`, `Illustrazione di …`, `Illustrazione non disponibile offline`;
  - Commander panel: `Nessun avversario disponibile`, `Selezionato`;
  - hub: `Tocca ◇ per strumenti e sezioni`, `Apri strumenti e sezioni`, `Chiudi strumenti`, `Sezioni`, `Dadi`, `Mana`, `Moneta`, `Torna agli strumenti`;
  - tools: `Lancia moneta`, `Testa`, `Croce`, `Azzera mana`, `Mana azzerato`.
- Targets:
  - every interactive area is at least 44×44px, including compact and rotated panels;
  - hub actions are 56px; dice buttons are at least 56×56px;
  - fallback 2×2 cells are at least 64×56px.
- Compact drawer: columns at least 52px wide and exactly `44 + 35 + 44 = 123px` tall; a 56px side rail. It applies when the card's usable height is below 200px (normative value since D-039; the spec first said 132px).
- Line icons only, monochrome, with no mana colours. State is never shown by colour alone: use a check mark, the text `Selezionato`, `Sì`/`—`, or fill.
- Transient surfaces never resize or move cards. Contatori and Danni Commander stay inside the card (`overflow: clip`) at every orientation. Only the Details side sheet may leave the card.
- Exactly one transient surface at a time. `Escape` closes the active one. While hub or tool is open, the player seats are `inert`.
- Motion (spec §10):

  | Event | Duration | Properties |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Radial | 180 / 140ms | opacity + max 8px radial shift |
  | Radial → tool | 140ms | crossfade |
  | Standard drawer | 180 / 140ms | opacity + translateY 10px |
  | Compact mode | 140ms | opacity |
  | Art change | 140ms | fade |
  | Dice result | 160ms | opacity + gold hairline |
  | Coin result | 220ms | flat fade |

  No animated rotation, overshoot, animated blur or layout change. Reduced motion: 0ms everywhere.
- Rules unchanged: the reducer and schema v5 are untouched. Commander damage ±1 moves life in the same action (D-006). Tax steps are ±2.
- Art (D-025): `art_crop` only, never full cards. The artist is credited. The random card's name is never presented as a commander. The app stays free; no new network calls outside `src/media/`.
- No new dependencies. Do not start R3 (Mazzi) or R4 (Impara).

## Review Focus

1. **Reload or go offline mid-game.** Every seat keeps the same random illustration: the envelope persists, its ids are in the prune keep-set, and nothing re-fetches. Pinned in Task 3.
2. **Remove a player (or their Partner) while a Commander panel lists them as a source.** The selection falls back to the first valid source with no announcement, and the open surface of a removed player closes with focus on the rhombus, never on `<body>`. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 7.
3. **Roll the same die and get the same value twice in a row.** It is announced both times. Pinned in Task 9.
4. **Rotate the device (390×844 ↔ 844×390) with the hub open.** The fit test re-runs, radial and 2×2 swap, and the layer never leaves the viewport. Pinned in Task 8.
5. **Start a new game offline.** It never waits on the network: after the single failed request, seats take spare cached arts, else the neutral texture. Online, the spares stay unused. Another tab starting a new game updates this tab's envelope. Pinned in Tasks 2 and 3.

### Task 3: Random art for seats without a commander

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/media/scryfall.ts`, `src/media/scryfall.test.ts`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/App.test.tsx`
- Create: `src/media/randomArt.ts`, `src/media/randomArt.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `MatchPresentation`, `assignArt`, `artKeepSet`, `startNewMatch` (Task 2); `ArtStore`, `fetchAndStoreArt`, `downloadArt`, `toCandidate`.
- Produces:
  - `fetchRandomCandidate(fetchImpl?): Promise<CommanderCandidate>` in `scryfall.ts`. It calls `GET ${API}/cards/random?q=${encodeURIComponent('has:art -is:digital')}` with the same Accept header, and throws `ScryfallError('response')` when `toCandidate` returns null.
  - In `randomArt.ts`:
    ```ts
    fillRandomArt(
      match: MatchPresentation,
      seatIds: string[],
      deps: {
        store: ArtStore
        fetchCandidate: () => Promise<CommanderCandidate>
        storeArt: (c: CommanderCandidate) => Promise<RandomArt>
        wait: (ms: number) => Promise<void>
      },
    ): Promise<MatchPresentation>
    ```
    - It returns the match with every listed seat holding art that is present in the store.
    - The identity of an assigned art is `(matchId, playerId)`.
    - Order per seat (spec §4.2, D-039 redline 1):
      1. the seat's own art for this match, if cached;
      2. a fresh network fetch: at most 3 tries per seat, skipping a `cardId` already used this match. All requests go through one sequential limiter with at least `SCRYFALL_MIN_INTERVAL_MS = 125` between requests;
      3. after a network error or unavailability, a cached spare not used by another seat;
      4. otherwise the seat stays unassigned, which means the neutral texture.
    - Online, `spareArt` is never used: a new game always looks for new art first.
    - The first network error stops all fetches for the run. The remaining seats then take spares, then the neutral texture, so offline costs one failed request in total.
    - The limiter `createRateLimiter(minIntervalMs, now, wait)` lives in `scryfall.ts`. It is shared by `fetchRandomCandidate` and `searchCommanders`.
  - `App` state `match`:
    - loaded with `loadMatch`;
    - saved on change;
    - after mount and after each confirmed new game, `fillRandomArt` runs for seats whose player has no `commanders[0]`;
    - a confirmed new game sets `match` to `startNewMatch(match)`;
    - **Azzera punteggi** leaves it unchanged;
    - the prune keep-set becomes commander ids ∪ `artKeepSet(match)`, and prune runs after `fillRandomArt` settles, never before.

    - maps a valid card;
    - throws `response` on a card without `art_crop`;
    - throws `network` when fetch rejects.
  - `fillRandomArt` with a fake store and a fake fetch:
    - keeps an assigned seat's cached art with zero fetches (Review Focus 1);
    - online with spares available: every seat gets a freshly fetched art, and `spareArt` is untouched;
    - never gives two seats the same `cardId`, retrying on a duplicate;

  - Rate limiter:
    - two calls in a row are spaced by at least 125ms with a fake clock;

    - confirming Nuova partita changes `matchId` in `localStorage[MATCH_KEY]`;
    - Azzera punteggi keeps it;
    - prune receives the envelope ids.

## Calls made in this plan (approved by ChatGPT on 2026-10-20, with redlines)

All of these are recorded in D-039.

1. **Spare art.**
   - `spareArt` holds at most 4 arts from earlier matches, kept in cache.
   - It is a fallback only, used after a network error or when the network is unavailable.
   - Online, a new game always fetches new art first.
2. **Seat identity.** `randomArtBySeat` is keyed by player id (`player-1..4`). The full identity of an assignment is `(matchId, playerId)`.
3. **Random card query.**
   - `/cards/random?q=has:art -is:digital`, at most 3 tries per seat.
   - Requests run sequentially through one shared rate limiter, at least 125ms apart.
   - Fetching stops at the first network error.
4. **Outside tap.** A transparent dismiss layer closes the hub or tool and consumes the gesture, so it never activates the card control underneath.
5. **Out of the game.**
   - «Fuori dalla partita» shows for every tracked defeat cause.
   - Details lists all causes.
   - The state clears by itself once a correction removes every cause.
6. **Temporary results.**
   - Dice and coin results clear on any exit from the tool.
   - Mana stays across tool closes and section changes. It resets only by hand, with a confirmed new game, or on reload.
7. **Compact drawer.** Compact mode is driven by a CSS size container query (`height < 132px`). Standard and compact share one DOM, so focus and accessible names never change with density.
