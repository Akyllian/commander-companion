# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

New and returning Commander players sharing one phone, tablet or desktop at an in-person table. The primary job is to update visible scores without interrupting play to remember thresholds or arithmetic.

## Product Purpose

Commander Companion is a clear, local-first score surface for two to four players. Success means a first-time player can start a game, change a total and find poison or Commander damage without an explanation.

## Positioning

The readable Commander table companion that teaches the important score categories while staying out of the game.

## Brand Personality

Arcane, luminous and composed. The fantasy atmosphere should feel like a useful enchanted instrument, not a decorative game launcher.

## Anti-references

Avoid parchment, illegible gothic labels, fake card frames, decorative particle storms, glassmorphism and controls that hide behind gestures.

## Design Principles

- The life total is always the strongest signal.
- Familiar controls earn trust; fantasy lives in light, line and atmosphere.
- Secondary rules are discoverable but do not crowd the scoring surface.
- Every table action works equally with touch, mouse and keyboard.
- Local-first behavior keeps setup fast and private.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep 44px touch targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, non-color status cues and a reduced-motion alternative.
